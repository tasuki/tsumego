Gokyo Seimyo (published in 1835) is considered a sequel of the well known classic Gokyo Shumyo (1812).

There are four volumes to this work:

  • Vol. 1 - Joseki diagrams
  • Vol. 2 - Invasion and Endgame diagrams
  • Vol. 3 - Problems
  • Vol. 4 - Games of Chinese Masters

This current series will focus on the problems from volume 3. The first 44 of them are titled "Tesuji Inside Territories" and typically capture opponent's stones guarding an area or make seki by exploiting their shortage of liberties. You can learn how liberties are tactically important by tackling these problems.
