Shikatsu Myoki Tsumego Series

PageType: Path     Keywords: Life & Death, Ko, Problem, Books & Publications

A problem series for the classic Shikatsu Myoki problem collection, written by Honinbo Shusai, first published in 1910 (120 problems). Contains 168 problems from a later edition organized into four sections.

Some problems, denoted by a "v", are variations and may have different stipulations from the parent. For example, #153 is a "White to make ko" problem, while its variation #154 is a "White to live" problem.
